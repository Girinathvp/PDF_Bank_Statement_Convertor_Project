Statement Converter

Bank statement PDFs to clean Excel ledgers

How it works

  1. Pick your bank

    Choose the bank that issued the statement. We also read the IFSC on page one and suggest the bank if it differs. Not listed? Use “Other bank” and the layout is worked out from the PDF itself.

  2. Add the statement

    Drop a PDF downloaded from net banking, or a scanned copy. Scanned pages are read with OCR and take longer, around 1–2 seconds per page.

  3. Convert

    Every page is read row by row. Dates, narration, cheque numbers, debits, credits and balance land in their own columns.

  4. Check the result

    We add up the debits and credits and compare them with the totals printed on the statement. Rows we could not read with confidence are highlighted in amber in the Excel file.

Good to know

  • Password-protected PDFs need to be unlocked first.
  • Files are deleted from the server within an hour.
  • Turn off “one sheet” to get each PDF page on its own Excel tab.
